##  About Me

I'm a 3rd-year B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, Coimbatore.

I build end-to-end AI systems — MLOps pipelines with drift detection, RAG-based evaluation tools, voice-driven applications, and full-stack deployments. I led the student team that built **VOX**, a voice-first examination platform designed for users with physical disabilities — presented at MindKraft 2026 (International Techno-Management Fest) and awarded the **Certificate of Leadership** by ATOM, Karunya.

I also led my team to the **Prototype Development Phase** of the AI for Bharat Hackathon — a national-level AWS-powered competition by Hack2Skill.

My work spans Python, FastAPI, React, TypeScript, Docker, MLflow, FAISS, and LLM APIs — with strong foundations in NLP, semantic embeddings, and model deployment.
